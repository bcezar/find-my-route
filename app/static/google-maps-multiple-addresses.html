Google Maps with Multiple Addresses — without the 10-stop limit

Google Maps accepts a maximum of 10 stops and does not automatically optimize the order. Find My Route solves both problems: it supports up to 5 stops on the free plan (or 50 on the Pro plan) and calculates the ideal sequence before opening in Maps.

Google Maps limitations for multiple stops

  • Maximum of 10 stops per route in the mobile app
  • No automatic optimization — the order is whatever you entered
  • No bulk import — each address must be typed manually
  • No progress tracking — there is no way to mark stops as completed

How Find My Route complements Google Maps

1
Add your addresses in Find My Route — up to 5 stops on the free plan, or up to 50 on the Pro plan. Manually or by importing a CSV spreadsheet.
2
Optimize the route — the TSP algorithm calculates the best sequence, saving distance and time.
3
Open in Google Maps — click "Open in Google Maps" and the route is loaded automatically with all points in optimized order.
4
Navigate normally — Google Maps guides you stop by stop with voice directions, avoiding traffic in real time.

What about Maps' 10-stop limit?

The link generated by Find My Route uses the Google Maps URL format that supports multiple waypoints. In practice, routes with more than 10 stops can be opened in the desktop browser without issues. In the mobile app, Google Maps may automatically split them into segments.

For stop-by-stop execution on mobile, we recommend using Find My Route's own Execution Mode, which navigates individually to each stop via Google Maps or Waze.

Does it work with Google Maps offline?

Yes. If you have the region's maps downloaded in Google Maps, navigation works even without internet. Find My Route only needs a connection for the initial route calculation.
