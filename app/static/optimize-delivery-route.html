Optimize Your Delivery Route

Delivery drivers, couriers, sales reps, and field teams: Find My Route calculates the ideal visit sequence so you cover less distance, spend less on fuel, and serve more customers in the same amount of time.

Why does delivery order matter?

A poorly ordered route can make you cross the city back and forth multiple times. Studies show that optimizing the delivery sequence can reduce total distance traveled by 20% to 40%.

That means:

  • Less fuel spent per route
  • Less vehicle wear and tear
  • More deliveries completed in the same time window
  • Fewer overtime hours
  • Customers served with shorter wait times

How to use it for delivery routes

1
Import your list — Paste addresses one by one or import directly from a CSV spreadsheet. Up to 5 stops on the free plan, up to 50 on the Pro plan.
2
Set the starting point — Enter the warehouse, garage, or departure address. You can also define where the route should end.
3
Optimize with one click — The algorithm calculates the best sequence in seconds, with distance and estimated time per leg.
4
Run it with Execution Mode — Navigate stop by stop, mark as delivered or skip closed addresses, all without leaving your phone.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Find My Route is optimized for mobile use. During the route, you can use Execution Mode — a dedicated screen that shows one stop at a time, with buttons to navigate via Google Maps or Waze, mark as delivered, or skip addresses that weren't found.

Who uses Find My Route?

  • E-commerce and marketplace delivery drivers
  • Couriers and gig economy drivers
  • Sales representatives with customer visits
  • Field technicians and service providers
  • Collection and distribution teams
  • Small businesses with their own delivery service
