Import Your Addresses via CSV or Excel

Have a spreadsheet with customer addresses or delivery points? Find My Route accepts CSV and Excel (.xlsx) files and imports all stops at once, with no manual typing required.

Accepted format

The file must have at least one column called address. You can also include a description column with the customer name, landmark, or any annotation.

CSV example:

address,description
"1000 Fifth Avenue, New York, NY","Customer A"
"350 Fifth Ave, New York, NY","Empire State Building"
"Broadway & W 42nd St, New York, NY",""

Step-by-step import guide

1
Open the menu — Click the menu icon (☰) in the upper left corner or click "⋮" (More options) in the stops section.
2
Select "Import CSV" or "Import XLS" — Choose the format of your file.
3
Select the file — Drag the file to the indicated area or click to select from your device.
4
Confirm the import — You will see how many addresses were found. Choose between "Add" (keep existing stops) or "Replace" (start over).

Don't have a file ready?

You can download a CSV template directly from the app. It already comes with the correct columns and filled-in examples. Just replace them with your addresses and re-import.

The template is available in the side menu under "Download CSV template" or in the import modal.

Supported delimiters

Find My Route automatically detects whether the CSV uses a comma (,) or semicolon (;) as the separator. No configuration needed.
